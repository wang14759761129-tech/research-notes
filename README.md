## Structure

- [papers/](papers/README.md): dated, source-linked notes on individual papers.
- [questions/](questions/README.md): questions connected to reading or practice.
- [templates/paper-note.md](templates/paper-note.md): a short critical-reading template.

## First reading task, after basic foundations

Choose one accessible paper directly related to the first sports question. Verify its title, authors, year, and DOI or publisher link. Write a short account of the question, sample, measurement, method, findings, and limitations. Clearly distinguish the authors' claims from your interpretation.

Do not upload paper PDFs unless redistribution is permitted. Link to the source and write your own notes. Mark whether only the abstract, selected sections, or the full paper has been read. English is the main output language; Chinese can support difficult concepts and open questions.

No reading quota is required. One understood note that changes a project decision is useful evidence.
